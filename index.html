01 / Research

A conversation between
electronics and the brain.

Building tools to connect neural activity across space and time—from individual cells to distributed circuits, and from fast dynamics to chronic studies.

01

Small footprints. More connections.

High-density interconnects patterned with electron-beam lithography, designed to expand the bandwidth of ultraflexible neural probes.

02

From one thread to many.

Automated, configurable implantation of multiple nanoelectronic thread probes to reach distributed brain circuits.

03

Interfaces that stay connected.

Integrated systems for chronic neural recording and stimulation, bringing together probes, implantation tools, and ASIC electronics.

A growing record of the work.

01EBLNanofabricated ultraflexible electrodes for chronic, high-density neural recording and stimulationManuscript draft · Nov 16, 2026+

Electron-beam lithography on polymer enables dense, ultraflexible neural interfaces for chronic recording and spatially selective stimulation. The latest manuscript covers probe fabrication and characterization, stable chronic recordings, bidirectional microstimulation, rodent behavior, and intraoperative human recordings. Explore the manuscript summary and all five main and fourteen supplemental figures, with their source captions.

Explore manuscript and all figures
02CentimaniPrecision robotics for parallel flexible-probe implantation.Manuscript in preparation+

Centimani: A precision robotic system for minimally invasive, large-scale, parallel implantation of flexible neural probes into rodents. The project brings together optical registration, coordinated software, and controlled implantation workflows. Manuscript in preparation; public figures and further details forthcoming.
