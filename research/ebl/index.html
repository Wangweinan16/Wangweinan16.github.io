Nanofabricated ultraflexible electrodes for chronic, high-density neural recording and stimulation

Weinan Wang, Yuhang Ma, Pavlo Zolotavin, Liuyang Sun, Hanqing Wang, Kyu Hyun Lee, Kalman A. Katlowitz, Carter A. Kelly, Hanlin Zhu, Rongkang Yin, Yingchu Sun, Robin Kim, Luis De La Palma Piedras, Jiaao Zhang, Yuxuan Liu, Xiaorong Zhang, Elizabeth A. Mickiewicz, Philip Adenekan, Nicole R. Provenza, Ganesh Rao, Alla Y. Karpova, Benjamin Y. Hayden, Sameer A. Sheth, Loren M. Frank, Lan Luan, Chong Xie.

We use electron-beam lithography to pattern dense interconnects on flexible polymer probes for chronic recording and stimulation.

Probe architecture and footprintFrom the probe to a metal traceRecording and tracking single neurons

Probe architecture and footprint

Electron-beam lithography fits more independently addressed sites onto a slender polymer shank. The layer structure and probe comparison show how the device achieves this density.

From the probe to a metal trace

Across three shank designs, the same contact, via, and trace geometry links each recording site to its own lead.

Recording and tracking single neurons

Densely spaced contacts sample the spatial signature of each spike. Repeated recordings show how those signatures and spike timing evolve across days.
