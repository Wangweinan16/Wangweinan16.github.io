Ultraflexible probes with a custom ASIC for 512-channel recording and 128-channel stimulation

Weinan Wang, Gerald Topalli, Xiaorong Zhang, Jiyuan Duan, Yiling Xie, Pavlo Zolotavin, Howard Wang, Lan Luan, Taiyun Chi, and Chong Xie.

This system pairs an ultraflexible four-shank probe with a custom ASIC for simultaneous neural recording and patterned stimulation. The figures follow the SfN poster’s progression from hardware and bench measurements to in-vivo results.

Integrated system and bench measurements

A four-shank ultraflexible array and custom ASIC combine 512 recording channels with 128 stimulation channels. Bench measurements characterize the assembled device.
