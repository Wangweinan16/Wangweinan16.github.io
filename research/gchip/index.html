GChip · SfN 2026 poster

Ultraflexible probes with a custom ASIC for 512-channel recording and 128-channel stimulation

Weinan Wang, Gerald Topalli, Xiaorong Zhang, Jiyuan Duan, Yiling Xie, Pavlo Zolotavin, Howard Wang, Lan Luan, Taiyun Chi, and Chong Xie.

Four ultraflexible shanks connect to a custom ASIC that records neural activity and delivers programmable current pulses. Bench measurements characterize the hardware, and two in-vivo recordings show how the system resolves single units and measures their responses to stimulation. Gerald Topalli of Taiyun Chi's lab developed the GChip ASIC.
