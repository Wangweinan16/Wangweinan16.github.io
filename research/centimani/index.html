Centimani · Manuscript in preparation

Parallel implantation of flexible neural electrodes

Centimani brings together independent positioning, optical targeting, and parallel insertion to place flexible electrode arrays across multiple brain regions.

Implanting threads in parallelTargeting the cortical surfaceRecording and stimulating across regions

Implanting threads in parallel

The robot positions many flexible threads independently so a distributed array can be implanted in parallel.

Targeting the cortical surface

Optical registration and site-specific height adjustments guide the needles to their intended entry sites.

Recording and stimulating across regions

Distributed electrodes reveal activity across regions and let us examine how neurons respond to stimulation delivered at selected sites.
