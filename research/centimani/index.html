Centimani · Manuscript in preparation

Parallel implantation of flexible neural electrodes

Centimani brings together independent positioning, optical targeting, and parallel insertion to place flexible electrode arrays across multiple brain regions. Weinan Wang built the robot in collaboration with Yu Wu, Romain Albouy, and Curtis Chen.

Positioning and delivering 32 threads

A portable cartridge couples the flexible array to the robot. Each needle can move independently before the threads advance together.

Optical targeting and placement

An optical overlay guides lateral positioning. Separate height adjustments account for the curvature of the cortical surface.

Recording across regions

Contact maps locate the recordings. The traces, unit metrics, and cross-correlations describe what the distributed array measured.

Mapping responses to stimulation

Single-contact stimulation links pulse timing and unit responses to the anatomy of the distributed interface.
